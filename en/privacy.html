Privacy Policy

As of November 9, 2026. Applies to the iOS app "Quadrino" by Silvio Lange.

In short

The app collects no data about you. There is no server run by the developer, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no sharing with third parties.

What the app stores

Profiles (name, color, picture, settings), solved puzzles and games in progress. This data lives on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs it between your devices through the private iCloud database of your Apple Account. Only your Apple Account can access it, not the developer. Apple's iCloud privacy terms apply. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device.

What the app does not do

No location, no access to contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Read-aloud uses your device's speech synthesis; nothing is recorded.

Deleting

Delete profiles and progress inside the app (parents' corner or settings). Removing the app and its iCloud data under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud removes everything.

Children

The app is also meant for children. Precisely for that reason, no personal data is collected or transmitted.
