One Sudoku for children and adults.

Children learn from a small robot what row, column and block mean. Adults play up to 9×9 expert in the same app, and the hints name the technique that gets you there yourself.

Two worlds in one app

When you create a profile, you choose child or adult. That decides the look, the help and the levels, separately for every profile.

For children

A robot explains, reads aloud and cheers along. It starts with 4×4; after five solved puzzles the next level opens.

  • Introduction at the first puzzle of every size
  • Animals instead of numbers for 4×4 and 6×6, if wanted
  • Stickers for first achievements
  • Parents' corner behind a small sum

For adults

Calm, in glass and metal. All sizes and four levels are open from the start, from easy to expert.

  • Notes that tidy themselves up as you place numbers
  • Time, best results and favourite time of day in the statistics
  • Puzzle of the day, the same for every profile

Hints that explain

The bulb doesn't just give away a number. First it names the technique and shows you where to look. If you're still stuck, you get the cell with the reasoning, and only then the number. It all counts as one hint.

Hard and expert puzzles can always be solved with these techniques. You never have to guess.

  • Naked single
  • Hidden single
  • Locked candidates
  • Naked pair
  • Hidden pair
  • X-Wing

Everyone plays with their own profile

Each family member has their own stars, their own levels and their own picture: 16 animals and characters for children, 16 objects in glass and metal for adults.

Progress lives in your private iCloud and is the same on iPhone and iPad. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device.
