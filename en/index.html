For children: a friend who thinks along

The little robot explains what row, column and block mean. It reads aloud, cheers along and stays friendly when a number doesn't fit. Puzzles turn into moments of success.

It starts with 4×4. After five solved puzzles the next level waits, all the way up to the big 9×9. Every achievement earns a sticker.

Play, learn, be proud. With no ads in between.

For adults: ten quiet minutes

And when you're stuck, the bulb doesn't just give away the number. It names the technique that gets you there yourself. You get better with every puzzle.

Questions

Is Quadrino really free of ads?

Yes. There are no ads, no ad networks and no analytics tools in the app. That won't change.

Do I need an account?

No. You create profiles in the app. If you're signed in to iCloud, Quadrino syncs through your private iCloud between your devices.

What age is it for?

Anyone who knows the numbers 1 to 4 can start with 4×4. If you like, Quadrino shows animals instead of numbers for 4×4 and 6×6. The robot guides step by step through the first puzzle of every size.

Is there a subscription?

No. €4.99 once, then everything is open, for the whole family through Family Sharing.

What happens to our data?

Nothing. The app collects no data. Profiles and progress live on your device and in your own iCloud; we as the developer see none of it.

Does it run on iPad?

Yes. On iPad in landscape the board sits on the left and the numbers on the right, which makes it especially pleasant to play.
