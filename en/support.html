Frequently asked

Why don't I see the robot?

The robot belongs to the children's look. A profile of type "Adult" plays without it. You can change the type in the parents' corner or in Settings under "Edit profile".

How do I unlock the next level?

Child profiles unlock the next level after five solved puzzles. Parents can also unlock sizes directly in the parents' corner. Adult profiles have everything unlocked from the start.

Why isn't my progress on my second device?

Both devices need to be signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and allow iCloud Drive for Quadrino. Syncing can take a few minutes. Without iCloud, progress stays on each device.

How do I delete everything?

In the parents' corner you can reset a profile's progress and delete the profile. To keep nothing at all, delete the app and its iCloud data under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.
